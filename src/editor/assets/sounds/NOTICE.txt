All the clips src/editor/sounds.ts registers are real recordings, only re-encoded (mono,
48kHz, loudness-normalised) to match this directory's convention. None are synthesised
placeholders — do not add one with ffmpeg; pull a real clip (Pixabay, Kenney's packs at
kenney.nl/assets, etc) and re-encode it instead.

editor-deselect.ogg, editor-create.ogg, editor-delete.ogg and editor-copy.ogg are from
"litupsubway" on Pixabay, Creative Commons / Pixabay Content License, no attribution
required. Longer and more textured than a typical UI blip (0.7-2s), so they're mixed
quiet and the retrigger floor on deselect is wide (overlapping itself reads as mush, not
feedback):

    litupsubway-ui-close-sfx-513359.mp3     -> editor-deselect.ogg  (deselect/clear)
    litupsubway-ui-open-sfx-513358.mp3      -> editor-create.ogg    (node created; reparent reuses this at neutral pitch)
    litupsubway-ui-trash-518621.mp3         -> editor-delete.ogg    (node deleted)
    litupsubway-key-collect-sfx-522219.mp3  -> editor-copy.ogg      (clipboard copy; also the "pick block" P-key action)

editor-select.ogg is "Mouse click.wav" by xtrgamr on freesound.org
(freesound.org/people/xtrgamr/sounds/257786/), Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY
4.0) — UNLIKE everything else in this directory, this one DOES require attribution; keep
the credit above (or link the freesound page) wherever the game's/editor's credits live.
A real mouse-click recording, pitched down (SELECT_BASE_DETUNE_CENTS in sounds.ts) so it
reads duller and less clacky, with a wider random pitch spread than deselect's
(SELECT_PITCH_VARIANCE_CENTS) so repeated clicks in a drag don't sound like one stuck
sample. Select is the single most frequent trigger in the editor (fires on every click
across all four select tools, inspect's click-to-pick, and box-select's anchor), so it
stays short rather than a litupsubway clip — those read as intrusive at that frequency.
Its retrigger floor (SELECT_MIN_GAP_MS) is correspondingly much tighter than deselect's.
Went through two earlier tries here (a synthesised placeholder, then a CC0 floraphonic
pop) — this is the one that stuck; if the attribution requirement is unwanted, swap back
to a CC0/Kenney clip instead.

editor-transform.ogg is ORPHANED: a leftover from a transform-commit sfx pass (drag
release, nudge/rotate/scale) that got ripped back out — no clip in the litupsubway set
fit it, and it was one of the noisiest offenders (fires on every drag release). Nothing
in sounds.ts references it; dead weight on disk, safe to delete whenever someone gets to
it. Its previous content was a Kenney "pluck_001" clip, in case transform-commit sfx
comes back and that's still the right feel.

editor-hotbar.ogg is ALSO ORPHANED, same story: hotbar slot-switch sfx (number keys,
scroll-wheel cycle) got tried and pulled back out, too much noise for something that
fires that often. Its content was "litupsubway-ui-equip-sfx-513361.mp3", in case hotbar
sfx comes back and that clip is still the right call.

editor-digging.ogg: "digging" by freesound_community on Pixabay (pixabay id 6691),
Creative Commons / Pixabay Content License, no attribution required. Cut to a 7s seamless
loop (its last 0.68s crossfaded over the head), mono, loudness-normalised to -18 LUFS,
for the elevation tool's stroke bed while it's still only projected ops, before the grid
is touched on release; its release fade (DIGGING_STOP_FADE_S in sounds.ts) is deliberately
longer than a UI clip's, so the loop winds down rather than cutting when the stroke ends.

Swapping any of these is a file drop: keep the filename, replace the bytes. The ids, the
play sites, and the mix live in src/editor/sounds.ts and don't depend on the clip's
contents.

The `editor-` prefix is not decoration: scripts/bongle-asset-rewrite.ts flattens every
asset dir to dist/assets/<path-after-last-/assets/>, so these land in the same
dist/assets/sounds/ as all 89 kit clips. Keep the prefix, or a name that collides with
one fails the build.
