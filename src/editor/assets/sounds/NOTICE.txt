PLACEHOLDERS (the two blips). Replace before this ships.

editor-select.ogg and editor-deselect.ogg are decaying sine blips generated with ffmpeg
for this repo. They are ours, under the same terms as the rest of the
engine source, and carry no third-party obligation. They exist so the
editor's selection sfx path is wired and testable, not because they sound
good.

The intended replacements are from Kenney's UI / interface audio packs,
which are CC0 (https://kenney.nl/assets - "Interface Sounds"). CC0 needs
no attribution and no share-alike, which is why they were picked over the
Minetest-derived clips in src/kit/assets/sounds (CC BY-SA 3.0). Keep it
that way: do NOT copy a kit clip in here to stand in, or this directory
inherits an obligation the NOTICE next door describes.

The `editor-` prefix is not decoration: scripts/bongle-asset-rewrite.ts
flattens every asset dir to dist/assets/<path-after-last-/assets/>, so
these land in the same dist/assets/sounds/ as all 89 kit clips. Keep the
prefix, or a name that collides with one fails the build.

Swapping them is a file drop. The ids, the play sites, and the mix live in
src/editor/sounds.ts and neither depends on the clip's contents; only keep
them short (roughly under 120ms), since a brush-select drag retriggers
`editor:select` for as long as it lasts.

editor-digging.ogg is NOT a placeholder: "digging" by freesound_community on Pixabay
(pixabay id 6691), Creative Commons / Pixabay Content License, no attribution
required. Cut to a 7s seamless loop (its last 0.68s crossfaded over the head),
mono, loudness-normalised to -18 LUFS, for the elevation tool's stroke bed.
